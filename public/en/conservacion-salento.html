Your tourism protects Salento

Salento is a territory that depends on responsible tourism. Your direct contribution funds the conservation of Cocora Valley, the maintenance of the main square and the preservation of the colonial church. No middlemen, no commissions.

Where does your contribution go?

🌳

Cocora Valley

Protection of wax palms, trail maintenance and preservation of the valley's ecosystem.

⛪

Colonial Church

Restoration and maintenance of the Iglesia Nuestra Señora del Carmen, Salento's historical heritage.

🏛️

Bolívar Square

Conservation of public spaces, urban furniture and green areas of the main square.

🏔️

Nature Trails

Signage, cleaning and conservation of trails connecting the town with the valley.
